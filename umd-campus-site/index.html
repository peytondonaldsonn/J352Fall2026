The Evolution of the University of Maryland

How the College Park campus has changed over the years

From Then to Now

Over the years, the University of Maryland has continued to expand, transforming its College Park campus into what students recognize today. From the construction of new academic buildings to changes in some of the university's most recognizable landmarks, the campus has evolved since its founding in 1856. While some buildings have remained part of the university for decades, others have been renovated or replaced to meet the needs of a growing student population. Looking back at the campus's history highlights its development and how the university continues to build on its past.
