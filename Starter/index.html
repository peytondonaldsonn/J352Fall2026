Background Information:

  • The company was founded in 2006 in Stockholm, Sweden, by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon.
  • Spotify officially launched in 2008, initially as a way to provide a legal alternative to music piracy and services such as Napster.
  • Spotify uses a freemium model, meaning users can access a free, ad-supported version or pay for a premium subscription with additional features.
  • Spotify has grown from primarily being a music platform into a broader audio company, expanding into podcasts in 2019 and audiobooks in 2022.
